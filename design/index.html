02 — Key Findings

What the Audit Revealed

Living Dental Health has zero AI-readable infrastructure — no Dentist schema, no FAQPage schema, no llms.txt, no agent.json. Every competitor also lacks llms.txt and agent.json — the AI readiness gap is market-wide, making first-mover advantage available right now.

Schema name inconsistency — existing schema identifies the business as "Living Health Dental" rather than "Living Dental Health." This mismatch actively undermines NAP consistency and sends conflicting entity signals to AI systems.

Yellow Pages listing exists under Dr. Andy Engel with the wrong phone number. An incorrect phone number in a citation directory is an active NAP consistency error that confuses both search engines and AI systems.

Apple Business Connect profile is unclaimed. As Siri and Apple Maps expand their role in local discovery, an unclaimed Apple Business Connect profile is a growing visibility gap.

The About page renders completely blank; dropdown navigation is broken. A blank About page not only fails patients — it fails crawlers. Google and AI systems cannot read what isn't there.

Despite these gaps, the practice has the strongest review foundation in the competitive set — 4.9 stars / 208 reviews. This is a powerful asset that becomes exponentially more valuable once properly structured with AggregateRating schema and an AI-native platform to carry it.

The platform itself (WordPress/Divi) is the root constraint. Patching schema onto a slow, bloated foundation cannot deliver the performance AI systems require.

03 — The Platform Problem

Why Patching the Existing Site Won't Work

Most digital agencies will look at this audit and recommend fixes: add schema, claim Apple Business Connect, fix the broken pages. Those fixes are necessary but insufficient. They treat symptoms while the root cause — the platform itself — goes unaddressed.

Living Dental Health's website runs on WordPress with the Divi page builder. This was a reasonable choice when it was built. But in 2026, it carries three structural limitations that no plugin or schema patch can overcome.

LimitationWhat It MeansWhy It Matters for AI
Speed CeilingWordPress/Divi sites typically score 40–65 on PageSpeed. Plugin overhead, render-blocking scripts, and shared hosting create a ceiling that optimization can improve but never eliminate.AI crawlers operate on token budgets. A slow site burns tokens on overhead before reaching content. Pages loading in 3–8 seconds get partially indexed. Pages under 1 second get fully indexed.
Templated SchemaYoast and Rank Math generate schema based on post type — every page gets the same generic block. No contextual awareness of actual content, no page-specific FAQ structure.AI systems use schema to build entity profiles. Templated schema produces a flat, generic signal. Contextual, stacked schema produces a rich entity signal that AI systems trust and cite.
No AI-Native ArchitectureWordPress was built for human readers in 2003. It has no native support for llms.txt, agent.json, or the structured content hierarchy AI crawlers expect.The next generation of AI devices and agents will route queries directly to businesses via agent.json. A platform that cannot support this file is structurally excluded from that channel.

The Bend Dental Market

Six primary competitors were audited across the same signal set. Each practice was evaluated for schema implementation, digital presence coverage, content strategy, and AI readiness. The pattern is consistent across all six: WordPress or Wix, partial or no schema, zero AI readiness files.

Dental Artistry
dental-artistry.com · Two locations · 592 Reviews · WordPress
Strongest

The strongest digital competitor. Has implemented Dentist, Service, and Organization schema with geo-coordinates and opening hours — more than any other practice in the market. However, a critical typo exists in their schema ("Venerrs" instead of "Veneers") appearing twice, their reviews page returns a 404 error, and they have no FAQPage schema, no Person schema, and no llms.txt. Their 28-post blog is the strongest content asset in the competitive set. Platform ceiling: WordPress.

Bend Dental Group

Freeman Smiles
