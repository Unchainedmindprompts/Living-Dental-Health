Having choices is a beautiful thing. Can you imagine if everyone in town wore the same clothes or drove the same car – or – gulp – didn’t have a choice in where to go for their healthcare? While we may not have multiple options for hospitals or cancer centers, we do have a lot of dentists, which means YOU have the power to choose the best dentist for you. 

Instead of feeling overwhelmed with the options, consider the ability to have a choice to be a blessing. Asking the right questions during your initial consultation (and we hope you have one with any dentist you are considering) can help you make an informed decision. Here are three of the most important questions to consider when choosing the best dentist in Bend, OR:

1.  **What is your preferred method of communication?** Communication is absolutely crucial when choosing a dentist. Your ability to communicate not only with the dentist but also with the staff is equally important. Here’s why:
    1.  Speaking the same language. Clear communication lets you understand the strange language your dentist may be saying when they tell you about a treatment they are recommending. Hey, you didn’t go to dental school and shouldn’t be expected to understand jargon related to the field. Ensure your dentist knows how to break it down so you can make an informed decision on your care.
    2.  Quality communication builds trust and long-term oral health. Your oral health is just that—your Oral Health. The treatment and care you receive are up to you. Having a dentist who welcomes open dialogue and questions is very important. Make sure any dentist you are considering is a fan. 
    3.  Better treatment outcomes. If your dentist glazes over what they want you to do to improve your oral health or provides limited clarity following a procedure, you may or may not comply. Clear communication ensures you are both on the same page, which improves compliance and treatment outcomes.  
    4.  Reduced anxiety. Dental anxiety is a very real problem for many people. A dentist who communicates effectively can help alleviate these anxieties by explaining procedures calmly and answering questions patiently. 
2.  **What is your policy on insurance and billing?** Unless money is no object, you likely want cost transparency. Understanding the dentist’s billing practices and their relationship with your insurance company (if you have one) can help you anticipate potential costs so you can budget accordingly and avoid unnecessary expenses. 
    1.  Understand whether they are in or out-of-network providers and any costs associated with regular dental services and potential procedures. We don’t recommend choosing a provider just because they are in-network, as the coverage for [out-of-network providers here in Bend for many common insurances is very similar to in-network](/articles/how-going-out-of-network-for-dental-care-may-save-your-teeth-and-wallet). ([Here’s more on navigating dental insurance.](/articles/navigating-dental-insurance-the-importance-of-choosing-the-right-dentist-over-an-insurance-plan)) 
    2.  Does the provider offer an [in-office dental savings plan](/patient-info) for people who do not have insurance but want coverage? These plans can be a great way to receive preventative dental care and a discount for other services at a competitive monthly price. 
    3.  Do they verify insurance coverage? Some dental offices leave it to the patient to verify insurance coverage and benefits. Get transparency on who will do what so you aren’t left in a lurch. 
    4.  Do they provide upfront estimates for treatments or procedures BEFORE they are done? If a treatment is covered at 50% versus 80%, out-of-pocket costs may significantly differ. Knowing before receiving services is critical and protects you against unexpected bills and potential financial strain.
3.  **What is your experience with \[insert concern here\]?** Not all dentists have the same experience, and if you have specific concerns, choosing a dentist equipped to help you is essential. For example, how they handle dental anxiety is very important to someone with it. If you are interested in having your whole family go to the same dentist, ensuring they are experienced in treating kids or teens is also important.

As mentioned, we highly recommend scheduling a consultation with any dentist you consider. Most dentists offer free initial consultations, and the best dentists in Bend, OR, will happily provide this service. You can meet the dentist during a consult, ask questions, and get a feel for their practice. 

By asking​​ questions about any dentist you are considering, you can find one who aligns with your needs and preferences, ensuring you receive the best possible dental care for yourself and your family.
